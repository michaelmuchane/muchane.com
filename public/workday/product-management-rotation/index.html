Product Management Rotation

Workday · ’21 – ’23

Acting PM for the mentorship experience in Workday Career Hub, taking a 96.2% non-engagement baseline through discovery, PRD, and a three-release roadmap.

Mentors was the first opportunity type displayed in Career Hub and still averaged 3.8% engagement across 91 customers. I diagnosed why through user feedback and usage data. Suggested mentors were strangers, and there was no informal way to test fit before committing to a formal mentorship request, which produced analysis paralysis. I defined personas, the problem statement, and success metrics, wrote the PRD, and built a three-release roadmap that prioritized a lightweight messaging path over heavier redesign options. A related UX feature I defined metrics for and iterated on achieved a 144% usage increase.

Receipts

  • 3.8% average engagement across 91 customers against a 96.2% non-engagement baseline.
  • Defined the conversion metric: mentor saves plus mentor requests over total unique suggestion views.
  • Success targets set: 50% of engaged users sending a mentor message, 10% lift across combined mentor engagement events.
  • PRD plus three-release roadmap with acceptance criteria, aligned on scope and resources with senior leadership.
  • Presented the metrics analysis and enhancement recommendations directly to the Talent Optimization PM team, who used it in release planning.
  • 144% usage increase on the related UX feature, iterated against first-launch benchmarks with Engineering and Product.
  • Second workstream: owned refinement and delivery planning for Workday Student enhancements unlocking the multi-institution market segment.
