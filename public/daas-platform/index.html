DaaS Platform

Raleigh, NC · 2025 – Present

A pre-seed data-as-a-service platform matching data supply and demand in a two-sided market. Client identity confidential.

Product lead for a pre-seed data platform. Took eight months of founder ideation to a scoped MVP, a fundable business case, and a working multimodal data application with human oversight built into the pipeline.

  • 7-step pipeline, 3 approval gates
  • $500K raise case
  • 160K+ addressable users

The client had a compelling vision and eight months of unstructured ideation, but no MVP definition, no delivery plan, and a hardware dependency that could sink v0 before it started. I converted the ideation backlog into a prioritized delivery plan across three sprints, framed the business case supporting a $500K raise, and drove the sensor decision by evaluating vendors on synchronization fidelity rather than marketing claims, because cross-stream sync was the one requirement that could not be shortcut.

The tradeoff I held firm on was making ML-proposed alignment a proposal, never an action: every offset carries confidence, method, and evidence behind an approval gate.

I also shipped clean, structured raw datasets first and deferred analytical insights entirely. Insights required data science capacity the venture did not have and would have delayed proof of the core value.

  • Translated 8 months of pre-existing ideation into a detailed delivery plan in 3 sprints.
  • Authored initial technical specifications and proof-of-concept requirements enabling an MVP to demonstrate market viability.
